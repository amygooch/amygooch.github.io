Thesis and dissertation

Interactive Non-photorealistic Technical Illustration

Amy Gooch. M.S. thesis, Department of Computer Science, University of Utah, December 1998. Advisors: Peter Shirley and Elaine Cohen.

The thesis takes conventions from technical illustration, silhouette and edge lines plus a cool-to-warm shading of the interior, and puts them in an interactive modeler for complex NURBS models.

PDF

Preserving Salience By Maintaining Perceptual Differences for Image Creation and Manipulation

Amy Ashurst Gooch. Ph.D. dissertation, Department of Computer Science, Northwestern University. The document is dated December 2006. Advisor: Jack Tumblin. Copyright Amy Ashurst Gooch, 2006.

The dissertation studies image-making that keeps perceived differences: a color-to-grayscale algorithm that preserves visible color changes, and a method for revealing an artist’s hidden revisions by comparing X-ray and visible-light images of paintings.
